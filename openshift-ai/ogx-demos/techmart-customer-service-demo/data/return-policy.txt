TechMart Return and Refund Policy

Shipping Information:
- Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days (Free on orders over $50)
- Express Shipping: 1-2 business days ($15.99)
- Overnight Shipping: Next business day ($29.99, order before 2 PM EST)
- Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days
- Tracking number sent via email once shipped

Return Time Limits:
- Standard items can be returned within 30 days of delivery
- Electronics must be returned within 15 days of delivery
- Opened software and personalized items cannot be returned

Return Conditions:
Items must be in original condition with original packaging intact. All accessories, manuals, and tags must be included. Items showing signs of use may receive partial refund or be rejected.

How to Return an Item:
1. Log into your TechMart account
2. Go to My Orders and select the order
3. Click Return Item and choose a reason
4. You will receive a return label via email within 24 hours
5. Pack the item securely and attach the return label
6. Drop off at any carrier location
7. Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the item

Refund Amounts:
- Full refund: Item returned within time limit, original condition, all components included
- Partial refund: Missing accessories (20% deduction), damaged packaging (10% deduction), signs of use (15-30% deduction)
- No refund: Returned after time limit, significantly damaged, missing major components

Defective or Wrong Items:
If you receive a defective item or wrong item, we will provide a full replacement within 90 days at no cost. Return shipping is free and there is no restocking fee. Contact customer service immediately.

Restocking Fees:
- Unopened items: No restocking fee
- Opened standard items: 10% restocking fee
- Opened electronics: 15% restocking fee
- Large items over 50 pounds: Additional $25 handling fee

Return Shipping Costs:
- Free return shipping for defective items, wrong items, or TechMart errors
- Customer pays return shipping for change of mind returns
- Standard return shipping: $5.99
- Large item return shipping: $15.99

Gift Returns:
Gifts can be returned for store credit within 60 days of original purchase. Original receipt is not required. Refund will be issued as a TechMart gift card.

Common Questions:
Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect your return.

Q: Can I exchange an item?
A: Yes, process a return for the original item and place a new order for the item you want.

Q: What if I lost my receipt?
A: We can look up your order using your email address or order number.

Q: Can I return an item without original packaging?
A: Items without original packaging may receive a 10% deduction or be rejected if packaging is essential.
